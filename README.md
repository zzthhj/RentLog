# Privacy Policy for RentLog

**Last Updated:** February 7, 2026

## 1. Overview
Your privacy is our absolute priority. **RentLog** is designed to be a **professional, 100% offline, and private** tool for landlords. We believe your business data—and your tenants' data—belongs solely to you.

**In short: We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. What happens on your device, stays on your device.**

## 2. Data Collection and Storage

### 2.1 Local Storage
All data you enter into RentLog is stored locally on your device. This includes:
* Property addresses and details.
* Tenant names and contact information.
* Lease agreements and PDF documents.
* Financial records (rent payments and expenses).
* Photos used for inspections and evidence.

**We (the developer) have absolutely no access to this data.** We do not have servers, and we do not transmit your data to the cloud.

### 2.2 Data Backup (User Controlled)
RentLog provides a "Backup & Restore" feature that allows you to export your data (as JSON or ZIP files).
* These backups are created only when **you manually initiate** them.
* You choose where to store these backups (e.g., your local Files app, iCloud Drive, or Dropbox).
* We cannot access or decrypt your backup files.

## 3. Device Permissions
To provide core features, RentLog may request access to certain device capabilities. These permissions are used **strictly locally**:

* **Camera:** Used to take photos for property inspections (e.g., documenting damages) and to scan documents.
* **Photo Library:** Used to import existing photos of your properties into the app.
* **Face ID / Touch ID:** (If enabled) Used solely to unlock the app locally for your security.

We never upload your photos or biometric data to any server.

## 4. No Tracking & No Ads
* **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics services (like Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel) to track your behavior.
* **No Advertising:** RentLog is a paid, premium utility. It contains **no advertisements** and does not share data with advertising networks.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the app.

## 5. CCPA & GDPR Compliance
We fully comply with the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** and **GDPR**.
* Since we do not collect, sell, or share any of your personal information, there is no data for you to opt-out of selling.
* **Tenant Privacy:** You, as the landlord, are the "data controller" of your tenants' data entered into the app. RentLog acts as a local tool to help you manage it securely.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
**Email:** rtx3070757@gmail.com

## 2. 數據收集與存儲

### 2.1 本地存儲
您在 RentLog 中輸入的所有數據均存儲在您的設備本地。這包括：
* 房產地址與詳情。
* 租客姓名與聯繫方式。

**我們（開發者）完全無法訪問這些數據。** 我們沒有服務器，也不會將您的數據傳輸到雲端。

### 2.2 數據備份（由用戶控制）
RentLog 提供「備份與恢復」功能，允許您導出數據（JSON 或 ZIP 格式）。

我們絕不會將您的照片或生物識別數據上傳至任何服務器。
